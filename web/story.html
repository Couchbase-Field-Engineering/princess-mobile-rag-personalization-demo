Chapter 1 · Life on board

A cruise is a week of moving in and out of coverage.

Guests make plans all day - but the moments they want to change them are often the moments they have no signal.

🏝️

Ashore and on excursions

Snorkel boats, Princess Cays beaches, zip lines and remote ports: no ship Wi-Fi, patchy cellular, and international roaming many guests switch off.

🛰️

At sea

Internet at sea comes over satellite, is sold as a package, and is shared by thousands of guests and crew. Plenty of guests never buy it.

🚢

Inside the ship

Steel decks, theaters, spas and interior staterooms: dead spots even when the ship is connected.

🧳

Terminal and tender

Embarkation-day crowds and tender boats: the exact moments guests open the app for their Medallion boarding pass and arrival group.

Olivia · Captain's Circle Elite · anniversary sailing

Olivia and Daniel are on a catamaran off St. Maarten. Their anniversary dinner is booked for 7:00. The boat is running an hour late. With no signal, she opens the app and moves the table to 8:30. It shows "On device". When the tender docks and the phone finds the ship's network, it syncs. The table is confirmed before they reach their stateroom.

What the app has to do in that moment

  • Show her reservation and the open seatings, from the phone
  • Accept the change and keep it safely, with no network
  • Send it on its own when a connection returns - no "try again later"
  • Let the cruise line apply it under its own business rules, and sync the result back
That is an offline-first database

Chapter 4 · Offline-first, step by step

Changing dinner from a catamaran.

The same tap in two apps: one with Couchbase Lite on the phone, one that depends on a live connection to a Redis-backed API.

1

Out of range

The catamaran is off St. Maarten. No ship Wi-Fi, roaming off.

2

Move the table to 8:30

Couchbase Lite: saved on the phone as a pending request. Itinerary, menus and open seatings all still load. Online-only: the request needs the server.

3

Back aboard

The phone finds the ship's network. The replicator pushes just the new document - no app open, no retry button.

4

Validated and applied

Sync Gateway checks it's this guest's own pending request. The backend runs reserve_specialty_dining as a governed AOM tool call.

5

Confirmed on the phone

The result, the booking and re-ranked offers sync back. Olivia sees "Confirmed".

Chapter 6 · Side by side

Couchbase vs Redis for an offline-first AI guest app.

Both are fast. The difference is how much of the app each one covers.

CapabilityCouchbaseRedis-centered stack
Database on the phone✓Couchbase Lite: embedded JSON database for iOS, Android and more, with SQL++ queries and vector search on the device.✗No on-device product.The app team adds SQLite or similar and designs its own schema.
Offline writes and sync✓Sync Gateway / Capella App Services: delta sync in both directions, automatic resume, peer-to-peer option.✗Build a sync API, change tracking and retry queues.Redis Data Integration feeds Redis from databases; it doesn't sync to devices.
Conflicts✓Built-in resolution, with custom resolvers where needed.✗Hand-written rules per data type.
Per-guest data on the device✓Channels and sync functions: a phone gets only its guest's documents, and can only create its own requests.~An access layer in the custom API.
JSON documents and queries✓SQL++ with indexes over the same JSON on server and phone.~JSON and the Redis Query Engine in the cloud; nothing shared with the phone.
Vector search✓On Couchbase Server and in Couchbase Lite.✓In Redis (cloud side).
Context and LLM caching✓Memory-first KV, plus AOM's Context Cache and LLM Cache on Couchbase - and cached context syncs to the phone.✓A strength: Redis caching and semantic caching (LangCache).Cloud-side only; it can't answer a guest who's offline.
Durable system of record✓Couchbase Server is the database and the cache.~Persistence and SSD tiering exist, but designs usually keep a primary database behind Redis and sync the two.
Governed AI actions✓Agent Operations Manager: identities, RBAC on tools, approvals, traces, audit, budgets.~A separate gateway and governance product.

Redis capabilities as publicly described by Redis: LangCache semantic caching, Redis Flex tiering. Couchbase: Couchbase Lite documentation.

A five-minute script

  1. Personalized from this morning's data. Sign in as Olivia. For You leads with check-in and a Premium Suite upgrade for her anniversary.
  2. A governed upgrade. Place a Princess Upgrade bid. It runs as an AOM tool call - show it in AOM's audit log.
  3. Go to sea. Profile → "At sea - no signal". The status bar shows No service.
  4. Change dinner offline. Reserve a table: it shows "On device". Ask the concierge something - it answers from Couchbase Lite.
  5. Back aboard. Turn the signal back on: the request syncs and is confirmed, and the concierge's playbook answers come from AOM's LLM cache.
